# Privacy Policy for BizManager

**Last Updated: August 28, 2026**

BizManager Technologies ("we", "us", or "our") built the BizManager app as a Commercial app. This SERVICE is provided by BizManager Technologies and is intended for use as is.

## 1. Information Collection and Use

BizManager is designed as an **offline-first** application. We prioritize your privacy and business data security.

### 1.1 Local Data Storage
All business data you input (including but not limited to products, sales, customers, suppliers, and accounting entries) is stored **locally on your device** in an encrypted or protected database. We do not have access to this data, and it is never transmitted to our servers.

### 1.2 Google Drive Backup (Optional)
If you choose to enable the Google Drive Backup feature, the app will upload a compressed ZIP file of your local database and associated images to your **own Google Drive account**. 
- This data is stored in a hidden "App Data" folder accessible only by BizManager.
- We do not have access to your Google account files beyond this specific folder.
- You can disable this at any time from the app settings.

### 1.3 Crash Reporting (Optional)
To improve the stability of our app, we use crash reporting tools. If enabled in settings:
- The app sends anonymous technical information about the crash (device model, OS version, stack trace).
- No personal business data is included in these reports.

## 2. Permissions
The app requires the following permissions to function:
- **Camera**: To scan barcodes and QR codes.
- **Storage/Media**: To save and restore backups and store product images.
- **Bluetooth**: To connect to thermal printers.
- **Internet**: Only for optional features like Update Checkers, Google Drive Backup, and Crash Reporting.

## 3. Data Safety
- We do not sell or share your business data with third parties.
- Since data is stored on your device, you are responsible for maintaining the security of your device and PIN.

## 4. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.

## 5. Contact Us
If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at support@bizmanager.app.
